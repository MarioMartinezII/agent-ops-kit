The morning digest prompt for your AI agent

Start the day with one screen: what to protect, what to prep, what to decide, and what to say no to. Works as a scheduled routine or a first-message-of-the-day prompt in Cursor, Grok Bot, Claude Projects, or any agent that reads your calendar and a task list.

Setup

  • When: daily, before your first work block (for example 7:30am local).
  • Inputs: your calendar for the next 24 hours, an Open Loops list, and up to 5 Priorities This Week.
  • Trigger: a schedule, or type Run morning digest.
  • Time to read: about 3 minutes.

Prompt

Run Morning Digest for today in [YOUR TIME ZONE].

Pull from: calendar (next 24h), Open Loops list, and Priorities This Week (max 5).

Output exactly these sections:
1) FOCUS BLOCKS: 2 to 3 time blocks I should protect (with times)
2) MEETINGS: each with purpose guess, prep needed? (Y/N), decision expected?
3) DECISIONS DUE TODAY: owner = me, with consequence if slipped
4) SAY NO / DEFER: meetings or tasks that conflict with priorities
5) OPEN LOOPS: top 5 by due date
6) RISKS: anything that can blow up this week if ignored

Rules: draft only; no sends; bullets only; flag missing inputs as BLOCKED.

Done when

  • The digest fits on one screen.
  • Every meeting has a prep Y/N.
  • There is at least one explicit say-no or defer suggestion (or the word "none").

Why "draft only" matters

An agent that reads your calendar should never accept, decline, or email on its own during week one. Keep it a reader and a drafter until it has earned more scope. The kit's hire checklist walks through that trial.
